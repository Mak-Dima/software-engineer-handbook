Availability. The probability a system is operational and accessible at any given moment. It measures total uptime regardless of failure frequency, provided recovery is rapid.

Core mechanisms to achieve high availability.
- Eliminate Single Points of Failure (SPOF). Ensure no single hardware or software component can cause system-wide failure.

- Redundancy. Replicate data and components. Use active-passive (standby) or active-active (simultaneous execution) configurations.

- Load Balancing. Distribute incoming network traffic across multiple servers to prevent overload on a single node.

- Health Checks and Failover. Continuously monitor node states. Automatically reroute traffic from failed instances to healthy ones.
 
- Geographic Distribution. Deploy infrastructure across multiple isolated data centers or availability zones to survive localized outages.
